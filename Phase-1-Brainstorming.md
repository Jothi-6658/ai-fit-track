# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
AI Fit Track

## Problem Identified
Many people find it difficult to track their daily fitness activities and maintain a healthy routine.

## Proposed Solution
AI Fit Track is a web-based fitness tracking application that helps users monitor BMI, workouts, exercises, daily steps, calories and water intake.

## Key Features
- User Registration and Login
- BMI Calculator
- Daily Workout Tracking
- Exercise Tracking
- Steps and Calories Tracking
- Water Intake Tracking
- Water Progress
- AI Fitness Recommendation
- Diet Recommendation
- Daily Calorie Goal

## Target Users
Students, beginners and people who want to monitor their daily fitness activities.

## Expected Benefit
The application provides a simple and accessible way for users to track their fitness activities and receive personalized recommendations.
